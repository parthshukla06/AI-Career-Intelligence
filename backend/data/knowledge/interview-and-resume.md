# Interview Preparation and Resume Guide

Interview preparation should combine programming practice, role-specific fundamentals, project walkthroughs, debugging exercises, and concise behavioral examples. Practice explaining decisions, tradeoffs, failures, and what you would improve.

A resume should be easy to scan. Use specific technologies already used, action-oriented descriptions, outcomes where supported, and separate projects from employment. Do not add metrics or achievements that did not happen. Tailor emphasis to the exact requirements of a target job.
